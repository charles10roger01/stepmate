Future Computer Proposal · Model

A model proposal: future shoes

This model is about shoes, not computers. Look at how it is built, then write about your own computer.

Proposal: StepMate Shoes

1 · Name and user22 words

My future shoes are called StepMate. They are for older people who live alone and still want to go out every day.

2 · The problem today28 words

Today, many older people are afraid of falling when they walk outside. If they fall, nobody may see them, and they cannot always call their family for help.

3 · Feature 1 + how it helps38 words

First, StepMate shoes can feel the ground. When the road is wet or uneven, the shoes will shake gently so that the user can slow down. This helps people walk more safely in the rain and at night.

4 · Feature 2 + how it helps39 words

Second, the shoes will connect to the user's phone. If the user falls, StepMate can send a message and the location to family members in five seconds. Users will be able to get help even when they cannot speak.

5 · Why it is better22 words

StepMate shoes are lighter and smarter than today's shoes. With them, older people can enjoy their walks and worry less about falling.

Total: 149 words

What to notice

  1. The writer starts with a person and a problem. The features come after the problem.
  2. Each feature has two sentences: what it does, then how it helps the user.
  3. First and Second tell the reader where each feature begins.
  4. The last part compares the shoes with today's shoes.

Do not copy these sentences. Use the same five parts and the useful language, but write about your computer and your user.
